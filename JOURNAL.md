Then I started on the PCB part, and this is what took me the longest. I had to design the board to the perfect shape of the Great Ball, and then I had to mark up all the pictures I found. I really thought it would take like 2 hours, but 7 hours are 7 hours well spent. 
## Board Design
I decided to make it shaped like a Great Ball, which sounded so cool in my head. Then I imported the Poké Ball DXF as the board outline. And then I spent probably 3 hours just trying to route all the USB wire lines without them crossing each other on a circular board. a CIRCULAR board. Why did I do that to myself? The wiring was a nightmare. In the end, I just decided to go with auto routing.

I started placing the USB ports around the edge like points on a clock. USB-C at the bottom, two USB-A ports on the left side, and two more at the top corners. And then I added Pikachu to the silkscreen on the back and Rayquaza on the front and Mewtwo and Charizard.
## Cool Effects
I flashed back to the time when Maggie did the hacker card, and there was a design of a golden thing on the board. I found the slideshow and found that you can put a copper layer and soldermask together to get that effect, which I did for Pikachu, Charizard, and Mewtwo. 

Pokemon USB Hub V1.0.0. I gave it a version number. I think that is cool.
